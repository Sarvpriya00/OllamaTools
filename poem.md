The clock hands turn, a silent, steady grace,
Marking the moments in time's endless chase.
A whisper of years, a breath on the pane,
Washing the window with soft, silver rain.

We build our lives on sand that shifts and flows,
Where yesterday's laughter softly grows.
The scent of old books, the echo of a name,
A flickering candle, a remembered flame.

Memory, a tapestry, richly spun,
With threads of sunlight and shadows begun.
We gather the moments, the joy and the sting,
The quiet resilience that passing years bring.

The river of time, it never slows its pace,
Carrying memories to a distant space.
We stand on the bank, watching the current glide,
Where all that was lived, and all that will abide.

For time is not lost, but merely transformed,
A quiet museum, forever adorned.
With portraits of souls and the whispers of might,
A constant unfolding toward morning's light.

And though the years rush by in a blur of gold,
The stories we carry are never too old.
They settle like dust on the heart's quiet shelf,
The enduring, beautiful self.
